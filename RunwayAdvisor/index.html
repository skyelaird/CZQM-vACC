CZQM Runway Advisor

Runway selection tool for CZQM / CZQX controllers — live METAR, wind components, MATS limits, and rate estimation

What This Tool Does

Fetches live METAR for CZQM/CZQX airports and computes wind components per runway. NAV CANADA MATS limits are enforced: 5 kt tailwind max, 30 kt crosswind dry / 15 kt wet. Out-of-tolerance runways are dashed and struck out. The tool estimates AAR (arrival rate) and ADR (departure rate) based on active runway roles and crossing dependencies. Capacity model mirrors atfm-tools for consistency across VATCAN systems.

Runway Selection

  • Click a runway card to cycle its role: OFF → A/D → ARR → DEP → OFF. Activating one end turns off the reciprocal (same physical strip).
  • Auto button picks the best config from a composite score: declared rate + max(0,headwind) × 0.5 + ILS bonus (+3 in IFR/LIFR) + preferred bonus (+10). Headwind bonus lets well-aligned lower-rate configs beat poorly-aligned higher-rate ones; ILS bonus gives precision-approach runways an edge when the ceiling is low.
  • Least-worst fallback: if all runways exceed MATS limits, the advisor picks the runway with the smallest combined tailwind + crosswind excess (tailwind weighted 2× since it's worse for stopping) and labels it "XX (over limits)" — you can't close an airport over a few knots of extra crosswind.
  • Dashed / struck-out = MATS-unavailable. Hover for the reason (TW or XW over limit).
  • ★ marks the airport's preferred runway (e.g. CYHZ 23, CYOW 07/25).

Badges

CAT II CAT III

Precision approach capability. Dimmed in VMC; lit when ceiling requires that category (≤200 ft → CAT II, ≤100 ft → CAT III). Non-CAT-II runways are blocked for arrivals when CAT II is required.

LAHSO

Land And Hold Short capable. Directional — only the runway that can physically hold short shows the badge (e.g. CYHZ 05 can hold short of 14/32, but not vice versa). Dimmed when the crossing strip is inactive; lit when a target runway is active.

Rate Box (AAR / ADR)

Below the runways, the rate box shows estimated AAR (green), ADR (blue), and total movements (purple). Rates come from a single-runway interleaved model:

  • Dedicated arrival runway: ~42–46 arr/hr (ROT-limited)
  • Dedicated departure runway: ~30 dep/hr (2-min successive floor)
  • A/D (single-runway interleaved): AAR from declared wake model (e.g. 22), but ADR is capped at 12/hr per FAA FOA Ch.10 §7 — a single runway doing both jobs can't deliver full declared dep rate alongside full arr rate. The 22 value would only apply to a dedicated-dep runway.
  • Crossing runways: parallel strips (Δhdg <15°) sum fully; crossing families get a 20% secondary discount (shared intersection time)

CYHZ-specific notes

CYHZ has ILS approaches on 23 (CAT I + CAT II) and 14 (CAT I):

  • 23 is the only runway capable of CAT II approaches (ceiling <200 ft). In LIFR, auto-propose gives 23 a +10 CAT II bonus so it wins even against higher-rate alternatives.
  • 14 is CAT I only — adequate in IFR when ceiling is ≥200 ft. Gets +3 ILS bonus in IFR/LIFR.
  • In VMC, 23 is the calm-wind preferred choice (+10 preferred bonus); wind alignment and rate still decide against strong-headwind alternatives.

Configs available:

  • 23 (ILS CAT I+II) / 05 — single-runway A/D, 22 arr / 12 dep. 23 preferred calm; 05/23 longer for heavy departures.
  • 14 (ILS) / 32 — single-runway A/D, 18 arr / 12 dep.
  • 14 Arr / 05 Dep, 14 Arr / 23 Dep, 32 Arr / 05 Dep — dependent configs giving heavy departures a longer runway while keeping ILS (or 32) arrivals. 22/22 with intersection dependency.
  • 05 LAHSO 14 / 32 — VMC + dry, 26 arr / 22 dep. Best-case combined rate but wx-gated.

Reading the Display

Airport Header

ICAO code, flight rules (VFR/MVFR/IFR/LIFR), and surface condition (DRY/WET)

Wind & Ceiling

Magnetic wind direction/speed, altimeter, ceiling & visibility inline

METAR

Raw METAR shown below each card (always visible)

Config Line

Active config name (e.g. “23 Single”) with CAT requirement if applicable

Wind Component Arrows

↓ 12

Headwind — favourable

↑ 4

Tailwind <5 kt — caution

↑ 8

Tailwind ≥5 kt — MATS limit exceeded

→ 6

Crosswind — arrow shows direction across runway

Crosswind Color Bands

≤12 kt

Light — generally acceptable

13–15 kt

Moderate — caution (wet limit zone)

>15 kt

Strong — may exceed wet limit (15 kt) or approach dry limit (30 kt)

Flight Rules

VFR MVFR IFR LIFR

Notes

  • METAR auto-refreshes every 15 minutes; manual refresh via the ↺ button
  • Wind directions are magnetic (18°W variation applied)
  • Gust factor is used for limit checks when present
  • VV (vertical visibility / indefinite ceiling) is parsed from METAR and treated as ceiling
  • Use “Adhoc METAR Request” at the bottom for airports not shown
  • Capacity + auto-propose logic kept in lockstep with atfm-tools v0.6.20+ so both systems give consistent rates for the same config
