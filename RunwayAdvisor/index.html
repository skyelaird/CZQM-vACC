CZQM Runway Advisor

Quick reference for runway selection based on current winds

What This Tool Does

Automatically fetches live METAR data for airports in the CZQM/CZQX FIRs and calculates wind components for each runway. The best runway (highlighted in green) has the most headwind and least crosswind.

Reading the Display

Airport Header

ICAO code and current flight rules (VFR/MVFR/IFR/LIFR)

Wind Info

Wind direction (magnetic) / speed in knots, plus altimeter setting

Wind Component Arrows

↓ 12

Headwind — Wind coming at you (good!)

↑ 4

Tailwind (mild) — Under 5 kts (caution)

↑ 8

Tailwind (strong) — 5+ kts (avoid)

→ 6

Crosswind — Arrow shows wind direction across runway

Crosswind Color Coding

≤12 kts

Light — Generally acceptable

13-15 kts

Moderate — Use caution

>15 kts

Strong — May exceed limits

Flight Rules Colors

VFR MVFR IFR LIFR

Notes

  • Data refreshes automatically every 15 minutes
  • Wind directions are magnetic (18°W variation applied)
  • Gust speeds are used when present
  • Click "METAR" on any card to see raw METAR text
  • Use "Adhoc METAR Request" for airports not shown
