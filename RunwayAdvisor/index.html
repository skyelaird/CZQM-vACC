CZQM Runway Advisor

Quick reference for runway selection based on current winds

What This Tool Does

Fetches live METAR for CZQM/CZQX airports and computes wind components per runway. MATS limits (5kt tailwind max, 25kt dry / 15kt wet crosswind) are enforced — out-of-tolerance runways are dashed and crossed out. The Auto button picks the best runway respecting wind, wet/dry surface, preferred-runway priority, and required approach category.

Interactive Runway Selection

  • Click a runway card to cycle its role: OFF → ARR → DEP → A/D → OFF. Activating one end automatically turns off the reciprocal (same physical strip).
  • Auto button re-runs the automatic selection at any time (e.g. after wind shifts or if you've over-ridden manually).
  • Dashed / struck-out runway means MATS-unavailable — tooltip shows the reason (TW or XW over limit).
  • ★ next to a runway number marks it as the airport's preferred runway — chosen by Auto when within tolerance (e.g. CYHZ 23).
  • CAT II / CAT III badge marks runways rated for precision approaches with lower minima — Auto falls back to these when ceiling forces CAT II+ (e.g. CYHZ 23, CYYT 10/28).

Reading the Display

Airport Header

ICAO code and current flight rules (VFR/MVFR/IFR/LIFR)

Wind Info

Wind direction (magnetic) / speed in knots, plus altimeter setting

Wind Component Arrows

↓ 12

Headwind — Wind coming at you (good!)

↑ 4

Tailwind (mild) — Under 5 kts (caution)

↑ 8

Tailwind (strong) — 5+ kts (avoid)

→ 6

Crosswind — Arrow shows wind direction across runway

Crosswind Color Coding

≤12 kts

Light — Generally acceptable

13-15 kts

Moderate — Use caution

>15 kts

Strong — May exceed limits

Flight Rules Colors

VFR MVFR IFR LIFR

Notes

  • Data refreshes automatically every 15 minutes
  • Wind directions are magnetic (18°W variation applied)
  • Gust speeds are used when present
  • Click "METAR" on any card to see raw METAR text
  • Use "Adhoc METAR Request" for airports not shown
